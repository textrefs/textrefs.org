The Board is the association's executive organ. Per the [statutes](/association/statutes/#art-11-board), the Board consists of at least two persons serving two-year terms on a volunteer basis ([Art. 15](/association/statutes/#art-15-volunteer-service-expenses-and-compensation)).

## Current members

- **Moritz Mähr** — [moritzmaehr.ch](https://moritzmaehr.ch), [@maehr](https://github.com/maehr). Researcher bridging digital history, science and technology studies, and open research infrastructure, with a focus on source criticism in the age of AI and digital sustainability.
- **Luz Christopher Seiberth** — Philosopher specialising in Kant, Sellars, metaontology, and category theory; organiser of the International Sellars Colloquium since 2014 and the Hegel Circle Berlin since 2019.

## We are looking for additional board members

TextRefs is a registry for canonical text references — Plato's Stephanus pages, Bekker numbers in Aristotle, biblical citations, Roman-law fragment numbers, mediaeval edition references, and many more. We would like the Board to reflect the breadth of disciplines that rely on these references.

We are actively seeking board members with domain expertise in:

- theology and religious studies,
- legal studies and the history of law,
- classical philology,
- philosophy,
- mediaeval studies,
- digital editions and textual criticism,
- library and information science as it relates to canonical references.

If you work with canonical references in your field and want to help shape an open, persistent registry, please reach out via the [contact page](/association/contact/). Board service is volunteer and unpaid; the [governance regulation](/association/governance/) describes the Board's responsibilities in detail.
