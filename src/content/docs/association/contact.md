## Email

General correspondence, board enquiries, code-of-conduct reports: <community@textrefs.org>.

## Other channels

- **Code of conduct concerns:** <community@textrefs.org> — see the [Code of Conduct](/community/code-of-conduct/).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do **not** email. Use GitHub's private advisory form — see the [Security Policy](/community/security/).
- **Bug reports, feature requests, mapping corrections:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/textrefs/textrefs.org/issues).
- **General questions:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/textrefs/textrefs.org/discussions).
